2. Score System

Base Score: +1 per tick while alive (survival reward)
Word Bonus: +10 × word_length per word typed correctly
Speed Multiplier: score × current_speed (faster = more points)
Skip Penalty: typing nothing = 0 bonus (but you survive)
Example: Easy mode, speed 1.0x
Type "leaf" (4 chars) → 10 × 4 × 1.0 = +40 points
Skip typing → +0 bonus (survive, but miss points)
Example: Hard mode, speed 2.0x
Type "adventure" (9 chars) → 10 × 9 × 2.0 = +180 points
This makes Hard mode genuinely rewarding, not just harder.

3. Plant Obstacle Types

🌵
Cactus
Tall, narrow. Appears as upper or lower. Classic obstacle.
🌿🔻
Bramble / Thorns
Spikes point DOWN from top. Wide, low-hanging threat. Upper-only.
🍄
Mushroom
Short, wide. Bottom only. Needs a small hop to clear.
🌱↕
Venus Flytrap
Wide jaws from bottom. Tall gap above. Upper+lower combo.
🌳
Tree Stump
Very wide, short. Bottom only. Forces a long low jump.
🌿⬇
Hanging Vines
Long, thin. Dangles from top with thorns. Upper-only.

Each plant type has different width/height ratios, creating varied gap shapes and jump requirements.

4. Start Screen Flow

① Title screen → Select Difficulty
Easy (3-5 char)  |  Medium (5-10 char)  |  Hard (10+ char)
② Click difficulty → Press SPACE or click START
③ Game begins → run, type, survive
④ Death → Score screen with word stats & WPM
⑤ Press SPACE → back to difficulty select
