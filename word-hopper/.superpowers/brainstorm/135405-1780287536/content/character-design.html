Pixel Frog
A hopping frog that jumps between words — thematic!

A: Pixel Frog

Frog fits the "hopper" theme perfectly. Recognizable silhouette. Big eyes, wide body, legs stretch when jumping. Color: teal body, yellow eyes, pink cheeks, dark green legs.

B: Pixel Runner

C: Pixel Bunny

White bunny with long ears, pink inner ears, red eyes. Ears go horizontal when jumping (aerodynamic!). Big feet. Most "hopper" of all options. Cute but determined. Very readable silhouette.
