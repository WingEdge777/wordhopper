A: Neon Pixel Night

Dark void background with neon glow on everything. Stars, pixel art obstacles with glow halos. Bright neon green player, cyan/yellow words. CRT scanlines. Feels like playing on a retro CRT monitor.

Pros

  • Most visually striking
  • Glow effects hide simple shapes
  • Classic arcade feel

Cons

  • Glow can strain eyes over time
  • Accessibility concerns (low contrast neon)

B: Pixel Nature Night

Moonlit outdoor scene. Gradient sky, distant hills, green ground with pixel flowers. Soft glow from moon. Obstacles are colorful pixel plants. Warmer, more charming and game-like. Less harsh on eyes.

Pros

  • More game-world atmosphere
  • Easier on eyes
  • Plants feel natural in setting
  • Moon provides subtle lighting logic

Cons

  • More background elements to render
  • Less "arcade machine" feel

C: Tron Terminal

Digital grid world. Neon blue grid floor, glowing wireframe obstacles. Cyan/magenta word colors. Feels like you're inside a computer program. Tron meets typing tutor. Very clean geometry.

Pros

  • Most unique identity
  • Obstacles = digital walls (easy to draw)
  • Typing = entering commands (thematic fit)
  • Grid floor is easy to render

Cons

  • Cold/emotional
  • Less "game" more "tool"
  • Blue-heavy can feel same-y
