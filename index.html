Word Hopper — Free Typing Practice Game

Word Hopper is a free browser typing game that combines word practice with a side-scrolling runner. Type the word on each obstacle, press Space near the green timing line to jump, and improve your WPM. Best played on desktop with a physical keyboard. Four difficulties: Chill, Easy, Medium, and Hard. Track local bests, climb the global leaderboard, and share your score.
